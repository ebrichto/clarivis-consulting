Accreditation & Compliance Consulting You Can Trust

Clarivis Consulting Group, LLC is a national consulting firm specializing in accreditation readiness, regulatory compliance, and strategic advisory services for health sector education programs. Led by Eric A. Brichto, Esq., Clarivis partners with institutions navigating complex accreditation and regulatory environments.

With deep experience across healthcare education, higher education accreditation, and regulatory policy, Clarivis provides practical, risk-aware guidance designed to support long-term institutional success.
