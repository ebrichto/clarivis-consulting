About Eric A. Brichto

Eric A. Brichto, Esq. is an experienced accreditation consultant and regulatory professional with more than a decade of leadership in healthcare and higher education accreditation. He is the Founder and Principal of Clarivis Consulting Group.

Professional Background

Eric previously served as Chief Accreditation Officer for the Commission on Accreditation of Healthcare Management Education (CAHME), where he oversaw accreditation operations, standards development, governance initiatives, and international program reviews.

Accreditation & Regulatory Expertise

Eric has advised hundreds of academic programs on accreditation strategy, regulatory compliance, and continuous improvement. His work includes developing new accreditation models, mentorship programs, and professional training initiatives across healthcare education.

Clarivis Consulting Group

Clarivis Consulting Group partners with public and private organizations to support accreditation readiness, compliance strategy, and operational excellence for health sector education programs.
