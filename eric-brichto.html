Who Is Eric A. Brichto?

Eric A. Brichto, Esq. is a nationally recognized accreditation consultant and regulatory compliance expert with more than a decade of leadership experience in healthcare and higher education accreditation. He is the Founder and Principal of Clarivis Consulting Group.

Accreditation & Regulatory Experience

Eric previously served as Chief Accreditation Officer for the Commission on Accreditation of Healthcare Management Education (CAHME), where he led accreditation operations, standards development, governance initiatives, and international program reviews.

Throughout his career, Eric has advised hundreds of academic institutions on accreditation strategy, compliance readiness, continuous improvement, and regulatory risk management.

Healthcare & Higher Education Accreditation Expertise

Eric A. Brichto specializes in healthcare accreditation, higher education accreditation, and regulatory compliance for academic programs. His work includes developing accreditation models, mentoring institutional leadership teams, and designing professional training initiatives.

Founder of Clarivis Consulting Group

As Founder of Clarivis Consulting Group, Eric partners with public and private organizations to support accreditation readiness, regulatory compliance, and operational excellence for health sector education programs.

Publications & Speaking

Eric is a frequent author and presenter on accreditation, quality assurance, and regulatory compliance, and is committed to helping institutions navigate complex accreditation processes with clarity and confidence.
