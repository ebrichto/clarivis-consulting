About Eric Brichto

Eric Brichto, Esq. is a nationally recognized accreditation consultant and regulatory compliance expert with over a decade of leadership in healthcare and higher education accreditation. He is the Founder and Principal of Clarivis Consulting Group.

Accreditation & Regulatory Experience

Eric previously served as Chief Accreditation Officer for the Commission on Accreditation of Healthcare Management Education (CAHME), overseeing accreditation operations, standards development, governance initiatives, and international program reviews.

He has advised hundreds of academic programs on accreditation strategy, compliance readiness, continuous improvement, and regulatory risk management.

Expertise in Healthcare & Higher Education

Eric specializes in healthcare accreditation, higher education accreditation, and regulatory compliance. His work includes designing new accreditation models, mentoring leadership teams, and creating professional training initiatives.

Founder of Clarivis Consulting Group

Through Clarivis Consulting Group, Eric partners with institutions to provide practical guidance in accreditation readiness, regulatory compliance, and strategic advisory services.

Publications & Speaking

Eric is a frequent author and presenter on accreditation, quality assurance, and regulatory compliance, helping institutions navigate complex processes with clarity and confidence.
